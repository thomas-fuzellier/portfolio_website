Desktop application that generates a tailored CV, a PDF cover letter and an Excel application tracker from a job offer:

-LLM pipeline: language detection, selection of the most relevant CV and pre-written paragraphs
-AI restricted to selecting validated content, with no generated text, for a fully controlled output
-Modular architecture, PDF and Excel export, unit tests with a mocked API
