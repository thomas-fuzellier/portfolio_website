
<span class="white-color bold">Engineering Internship – Embedded Systems & Instrumentation</span>
<span class="light-blue-color bold">Stratolia</span> <span class="light-blue-color">Palaiseau, France</span> June - August 2023

Project focused on the design and development of embedded systems for Earth observation using a stratospheric balloon.

-Development of a ground control system for an onboard camera
-Mechanical design and prototyping of a stratospheric payload gondola
-Embedded development on microcontrollers for sensor data acquisition
-Electronic design and PCB development
-Design and implementation of a test bench to simulate flight conditions
